Analysis paragraph

Life for Mexican immigrants has changed in many ways. In the early 20th century, most worked in farms and faced strong discrimination. Laws often limited their rights. In the early 21st century, many immigrants work in different jobs and build communities. They still face challenges, like debates about immigration and border control. American attitudes have shifted over time. Some people welcome immigrants and value their culture. Others remain against immigration. What has stayed the same is the struggle for respect and fair treatment. Mexican immigrants continue to contribute to American society while seeking opportunity and equality.
