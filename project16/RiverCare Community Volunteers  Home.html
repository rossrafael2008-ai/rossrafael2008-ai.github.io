RiverCare Community Volunteers is a local group dedicated to cleaning, restoring, and protecting the Greenway River. We organize monthly cleanups, educational workshops, and community events.

Our mission, vision, and values

RiverCare Community Volunteers was founded in 2015 by a small group of neighbors who were concerned about increasing pollution in the Greenway River. Since then, we have grown into a network of over 200 active volunteers.

Mission

To restore and protect the Greenway River through hands-on cleanups, education, and community advocacy.

Vision

A thriving, clean river that supports wildlife, recreation, and a healthy community for generations.

Core values

  • Community collaboration
  • Environmental stewardship
  • Education and empowerment
  • Transparency and accountability

Project highlight: Habitat Restoration Week

During Habitat Restoration Week, volunteers worked with local biologists to plant native trees, remove invasive plants, and stabilize eroding riverbanks.

  • Key outcomes
    • 120 native trees planted
    • 400 meters of riverbank stabilized
    • Invasive species removed:
      • Japanese knotweed
      • Himalayan balsam
  • Partners
    • Greenway City Council
    • Local high school environmental club

Upcoming events

All events are family-friendly and open to new volunteers. Please register through the Join & Contact page so we can prepare enough supplies.

RiverCare Upcoming Events
Date Event Location Difficulty What to bring

How to prepare for events

  1. Register in advance using our online form.
  2. Check the weather and dress appropriately.
  3. Bring a reusable water bottle and snacks.
  4. Arrive 10–15 minutes early for orientation.
