Core instructions

Having more knowledge of search engine optimization (SEO), you can see how some of your favorite websites keep SEO techniques in mind when designing their pages. And maybe some of them miss out on opportunities to rank higher in search results!

That’s exactly what you’re going to investigate in this assignment. You will explore three websites you already visit regularly and examine how they use SEO methods — and how they could do better.

What you will analyze

Think of this as a guided “SEO concept illustration” using real-world examples. You will:

  • Identify specific SEO methods being used on each site.
  • Describe how those methods contribute to search engine rankings and user experience.
  • Suggest additional ways each site could improve its SEO.
Choosing your websites
  • Avoid extremely popular, global sites (they’re complex and harder to evaluate).
  • Avoid sites that serve a singular purpose (for example, only video sharing).
  • Prefer sites you use often for information, shopping, or services.
  • Pick three different types of websites if possible (for example, news, e-commerce, blog).

Turn it in

Submission guidance

Your completed work will be graded using two major criteria: how well you identify SEO on each website and how clearly you propose potential SEO improvements for those sites.

For each website, carefully examine how the page is built and how information is presented. Your goal is to describe the SEO methods that are already in place.

Look for things like:

  • Clear, descriptive page titles and meta descriptions.
  • Logical heading structure (H1, H2, H3) that matches the page’s main topics.
  • Readable, keyword-focused content that feels natural instead of stuffed.
  • Internal links that help you navigate to related pages.
  • Clean, user-friendly layout that keeps visitors on the site longer.

When you write your answers, be specific: point to real examples from the site (for example, “The home page uses an H1 that clearly states the main topic”).

Next, imagine you are an SEO consultant for each site. Based on what you see, explain how the website could improve its SEO.

Consider improvements such as:

  • Making titles and headings more descriptive and aligned with user search intent.
  • Adding or improving meta descriptions to summarize each page clearly.
  • Rewriting content to be more focused, readable, and keyword-informed.
  • Strengthening internal linking between related articles or product pages.
  • Improving navigation, page speed, or mobile friendliness to support better SEO.

Your suggestions should connect back to specific examples (for example, “The ‘Contact’ page has no meta description; adding one would help users understand what they’ll find there from search results.”).

Use your worksheet to organize your observations and suggestions for the three websites you selected. When you’re finished, follow your course’s instructions to submit your completed worksheet.

How you’ll be evaluated

Your work will be evaluated based on how clearly you identify existing SEO methods and how thoughtfully you propose additional SEO strategies for each website.

Use this rubric as a checklist before you submit. Ask yourself: Have I clearly described the SEO methods being used, and have I thoughtfully explained how each site could better optimize for search engines?

When in doubt, look at each page through two lenses: “Can a search engine understand this?” and “Would a real person enjoy using this?”
